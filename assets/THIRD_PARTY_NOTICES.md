# Third-party app icons

Mass Installer bundles selected logo shapes from **Simple Icons 16.27.1** so the app never needs to request icons at runtime.

- Project: https://simpleicons.org/
- Package source: https://www.npmjs.com/package/simple-icons/v/16.27.1
- General Simple Icons license: CC0-1.0
- Local modification: SVG fills were changed to `#ededed` for this dark interface.

Individual brands and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. Inclusion does not imply endorsement.

- `vivaldi.svg` — CC-BY-4.0 — source: https://vivaldi.com/press
- `git.svg` — CC-BY-3.0 — source: https://git-scm.com/community/logos
- `gimp.svg` — CC-BY-SA-4.0 — source: https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/gimp-data/-/blob/e62f1905dcef7ec831ea6c052bb035eb37eaa69e/images/logo/gimp-logo-unicolor.svg
